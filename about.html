About

The College of Engineering Chengannur (CEC) stands as a premier technical institution in Kerala, widely recognized for its strong academic foundation and vibrant campus culture. Established in 1993 under the auspices of the Institute of Human Resources Development (IHRD), a government of Kerala undertaking, the college has spent over three decades shaping skilled engineering professionals. It is permanently affiliated with the APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU) and approved by the AICTE. Nestled in the heart of Chengannur, Alappuzha, the campus is known for its rigorous training in core and emerging tech sectors, particularly through its highly sought-after undergraduate programs in fields like Computer Science, Electronics, and Electrical engineering. Beyond core academics, CEC boasts a highly active student community driven by prominent global technical bodies like IEEE, ISTE, and CSI, which foster innovation, hackathons, and real-world project development. Combined with a stellar track record of placements in top-tier multinational companies and a strong, globally dispersed alumni network, the college remains a top choice for engineering aspirants in the region.
